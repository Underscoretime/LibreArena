How is this computed?

Each eligible benchmark is worth 1 point. Per task: 0.7 × auto pass-rate + 0.3 × human score (human 1–5 mapped to 0–1); auto-only or human-only tasks use what exists. Missing tasks count as 0. Total = sum, so with 10 benchmarks the scale is 0–10. Models covering fewer than half the benchmarks are marked ~provisional. Embedding models are excluded.
